AGILE - SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

Agile is a software development approach that focuses on
iterative development, continuous feedback, collaboration,
and delivering working software frequently.

Agile allows teams to respond to changing requirements and
customer feedback throughout the development process.

SCRUM

Scrum is a popular Agile framework used to manage software
development projects.

SPRINT

A Sprint is a fixed period during which a team works on a
selected set of tasks and delivers a usable increment of the
product.

A Sprint commonly lasts between one and four weeks.

PRODUCT BACKLOG

The Product Backlog is a prioritized list of features,
requirements, improvements, and bug fixes that may be needed
in the product.

SPRINT BACKLOG

The Sprint Backlog contains the work selected from the Product
Backlog that the team plans to complete during the current
Sprint.

USER STORY

A User Story describes a requirement from the user's perspective.

A common format is:

As a user, I want to perform an action so that I can achieve
a specific goal.

DAILY SCRUM

Daily Scrum is a short daily meeting where team members
discuss their progress, upcoming work, and any impediments.

SPRINT REVIEW

Sprint Review is conducted at the end of a Sprint to demonstrate
the completed work and collect feedback from stakeholders.

SPRINT RETROSPECTIVE

Sprint Retrospective is a meeting where the team discusses
what went well, what could be improved, and what actions should
be taken in the next Sprint.

DEFINITION OF DONE

Definition of Done is a shared understanding of the conditions
that must be satisfied for a piece of work to be considered
complete.

AGILE TESTING

In Agile development, testing is performed continuously
throughout the development lifecycle rather than being left
until the end.

Testers work closely with developers and other team members
to identify defects early and provide continuous feedback.

Agile promotes collaboration, adaptability, continuous
improvement, and frequent delivery of working software.
